# Scenario card

You're helping out the purchasing team at **Contoso**, a (fictional) company whose US branch is set up in Dynamics 365 as legal entity **USMF**.

This morning, a colleague sent you this short email:

> **From:** Sam, Warehouse Team
> **Subject:** Can you order some speakers?
>
> Hi, we're running low on mid-range speakers. Could you raise a purchase order with Fabrikam Supplier (vendor US-104) for 10 units of item M0001? The agreed price is 25.00 USD each. Please have them delivered to site 1, warehouse 11.
> Thanks, Sam

## The order at a glance

This is what the finished purchase order should contain. You'll use this table to check Copilot's preview.

| What | Value |
|---|---|
| Legal entity | USMF |
| Vendor | US-104 (Fabrikam Supplier) |
| Item | M0001 (mid-range speaker) |
| Quantity | 10 |
| Unit price | 25.00 USD |
| Site / warehouse | 1 / 11 |

That's all the data you need. Next, open `exercise-guide.md`.

---

<sub>Facilitator note: these values come from the standard Contoso demo data. Before the session, check that vendor US-104 and item M0001 exist in legal entity USMF in your sandbox, and update this card if yours differ.</sub>
